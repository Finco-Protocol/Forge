## How this dossier was built

Every claim was derived from the source and committed records at that commit; older documents were treated as evidence to check, not as truth. Where one contradicted the source, the dossier states the source truth and the disagreement is in [`KNOWN-DISCREPANCIES.md`](KNOWN-DISCREPANCIES.md). Evidence tags used throughout:

- **SOURCE**: read in tracked files at the commit.
- **RAN**: a command executed while preparing the dossier (listed with results in [`REPRODUCTION.md`](REPRODUCTION.md) 10.8).
- **DOC**: stated by a repository document and not re-verified here.
- **CI-ONLY**: needs network/Docker/PostgreSQL; run by CI according to the repository, not re-run here.
- **NOT VERIFIED**: cannot be established from the repository; stated as such rather than guessed.

## Ten facts to hold in mind before reading the code

1. The Forge stack is the recovered **deploy-time PONS V2 source** plus one factory change (**M-ELIG**: every launch needs a creator-consent signature and an eligibility attestation, checked by an immutable `ForgeLaunchAuthorizer` inside the factory's private `_launchToken`) plus five additive contracts under `contractsV2-forge/src/v2/forge/`. Curve maths, fees, graduation, hook, escrow, vault, locker, deployer and executor are byte-identical to the deploy-time tree.
2. **Highest-value review targets, in order:** `ForgeProtocolRevenueSplitter` (685 lines), `ForgeLaunchAuthorizer` (458), `ForgeBindingRegistry` (322), the factory's M-ELIG diff (49 diff lines) and its interaction with `msg.data` decoding, then the unmodified PONS contracts under Forge's wiring.
3. **Revenue provenance is attested, not proven.** The canonical escrow lets anyone credit any key; the splitter only pulls up to a ceiling signed by a trusted off-chain attestor, itself bounded on chain by `escrowClaimed + held`.
4. **Standing owner powers remain** (F-03 creator-recipient override after 3 d + 3 d; F-04 rescues with no delay except the 7 d graduation rescue). They are disclosed, monitored by alerts, and accepted by nobody.
5. Every owner action other than the few explicit timelocks is **immediate**; under a timelocked owner it inherits the owner's delay, including the emergency stops.
6. `FINCO_BURN_ENABLED` is `false`; the "10" of 70/20/10 is a **reserve liability with no exit**, not a burn. The PONS buyback vault **vests** tokens; it does not burn them.
7. The factory has **106 B** of EIP-170 headroom (24,470 of 24,576 B); the splitter runtime is **11,680 B**.
8. Chain 4663's EVM `block.number` is the parent-chain number; the splitter reads evidence through **ArbSys** with a 256-block window. Steps 7-9 of the lifecycle (attestation, treasury claim, creator claim) are **NOT VERIFIED** on a fork of the live chain (`A07e`).
9. The scanner baseline (302 findings) is agent-proposed and **UNREVIEWED**. 25 of its high-severity findings are in unmodified PONS code and are deferred to this audit.
10. BUSL-1.1 code (`Pool.sol`, `Position.sol` from Uniswap v4-core) is compiled into the graduation guard; legal review is open.
