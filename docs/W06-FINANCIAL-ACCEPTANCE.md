## 2. How the independent regression works (`U`)

- **World generator** (own seeded PRNG, 12 fixed seeds): four launches — two at the eligible 30% snapshot, one at 50% (ineligible share), one whose protocol leg is credited to a different recipient — and a random sequence of trades (fee multiples of 10, random creator tax), sweeps, third-party credits to the splitter key and to creators, launch fees, owner rescues (F-04), direct donations, honest-attestor ceiling raises + pulls with cumulative 2:1 rounding, treasury claims and creator claims.
- **Oracle**: plain `bigint` bookkeeping inside the test (30% = `3F/10`, creator base `7F/10`, reserve `floor(pulled/3)`); it never calls ledger code.
- **Checks**: after **every step** the prefix of events is fed to `buildLedger` and compared with the oracle (verified eligible revenue, per-token protocol/creator/tax credits, creator escrow balances, splitter escrow balance, launch-fee/rescue/donation classes, allocation sums), plus the invariants F1–F7 above.
- **Non-vacuity**: a coverage test asserts the 12 seeds together contain every event type and non-zero pulled / tax / third-party totals. **Mutation check (done, then reverted):** removing the `recipient !== splitter` guard in `verifiedEligibleRevenue` fails the property on several seeds.

## 4. What this does **not** prove

- Provenance of third-party escrow credits is still *attested, not proven on chain* (`ESCROW-CREDIT`).
- Real PostgreSQL ledger scale/behaviour (`pnpm test:pg` is opt-in and was not run here).
- Production splitter/escrow addresses, KMS custody, governance and an external audit.
- The synthetic generator models the implemented event shapes; a contract that emits different shapes would not be caught here (the on-chain `economics` suite covers the real emission).
